Nordin sermon notes — sorted for ingest
=======================================

These PDFs are a copy of the Drive zip, split so you can download them
to your PC and ingest only the good set.

This folder is for download only. Do not merge it into development —
the app does not load sermons from GitHub, and these binaries would
bloat the repo.

How to get them onto your PC
----------------------------
On GitHub: open this branch/PR → sermon-notes/ → download the folder
you want (or clone the branch and copy sermon-notes/ to your Desktop).

Folders
-------
00-already-in-corpus/  NKJV + the three books (already in RAG). Do not re-ingest.
01-ingest-first/       ~42 pastoral/core sermons. Upload these first.
02-ingest-second/      Remaining real sermons. Add after wave 1 is working.
03-skip/               Funerals, worksheets, guest speakers, empty one-pagers,
                       Cover-to-Cover class notes, duplicates.

Ingest with “Keep out of sermon library” only for books / private notes.
Do not ingest 03-skip.

WEDDING CHAIN BREAKERS 2017.pdf was not in the zip (only CHAIN BREAKERS.pdf).
