Dropbox notes

Walk through the Word study notes stay in Dropbox until they are downloaded here. Each PDF/DOCX is ingested, then attached to the matching video by sermon date (for example January 4.pdf with Vimeo title “January 4”) or by a Vimeo id in the filename.

Configure DROPBOX_ACCESS_TOKEN and either DROPBOX_NOTES_FOLDER or DROPBOX_SHARED_URL in tokens.env, then run bash apply-tokens.sh. Progress appears in Ingestion Jobs. After ingest, Premium members can open the notes from the Media page.
