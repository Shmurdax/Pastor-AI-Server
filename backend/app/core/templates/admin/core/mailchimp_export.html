Mailchimp audience

Collects login emails from Nordin's AI accounts and upserts them into the existing Mailchimp audience. Staff then send event and announcement campaigns from Mailchimp. Contacts are tagged nordin-ai. Existing unsubscribes are left alone.

You do not need the Mailchimp login to confirm this. The connection line below means the API key can read that audience. Export counts come from Mailchimp's own responses. Check a sample reads those emails back and shows whether each one is subscribed and tagged.
